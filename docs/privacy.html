Comeback Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Comeback does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • The game works fully offline and makes no network requests.
  • It contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • Your progress (scores, gems, upgrades, quests and sound setting) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • The game has no accounts and no purchases.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

Tap to throw. It always comes back.

Comeback is a one-finger arcade game you can learn in five seconds. You stand in the middle of the arena, the swarm closes in from every side, and your boomerang is all you have. Tap where you want it to fly. Hold to keep throwing.

- HITS ON THE WAY BACK SCORE DOUBLE. Line up a throw that catches them going out and coming home.
- LEVEL UP EVERY FEW SECONDS. Pick one of three upgrades: extra boomerangs, splinters, a guardian blade, chill, catch pulses and more.
- NEW THREATS AS YOU GO. Darts, weavers, tanks, splitters, closing rings, and a boss every fifth wave.
- ONE FREE COMEBACK PER RUN. Knocked out? You get back up once with a screen-clearing blast.
- KEEP WHAT YOU EARN. Gems buy permanent upgrades, and rotating quests pay out more.

No ads. No purchases. No account. Plays offline, in portrait or landscape, on iPhone and iPad.
